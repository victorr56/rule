# MaisonReceipts

Source: https://www.group-ib.com/blog/fake-receipts-generators/

MaisonReceipts is a fraud-as-a-service platform that allows users to generate fake receipts for over 21 major brands. The service is promoted on social media and uses Discord for customer support and community building. Users purchase subscriptions to create customizable receipts in various currencies and formats (US, UK, EU) to facilitate the resale of counterfeit or stolen goods.

- **MaisonReceipts_Fraud_Service_Domain** (sigma, high): This rule keys on dedicated, high-specificity domains used by the MaisonReceipts fraud platform.
